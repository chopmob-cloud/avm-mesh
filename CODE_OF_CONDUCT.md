# Code of conduct

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where someone is
representing this project.

## The standard

Be straightforward about technical claims and decent to the person making them. You can say
a design is wrong, that an argument does not hold, or that evidence is missing — that is the
point of the project. You cannot make it about the person who wrote it.

Specifically welcome: being told you are wrong with a reproduction attached. That is the
most useful message this repository receives, and the fastest way to get a fix.

Specifically not welcome: harassment, personal attacks, dismissive pile-ons, publishing
someone's private information, or sustained disruption of a thread.

## On disagreement about claims

This project's whole purpose is that assertions should be checkable, so argument about
evidence is expected and healthy. The rule is that the disagreement stays on the artefact:
what was run, what came back, what it does and does not prove. "You have not shown that" is
a legitimate and welcome contribution; "you clearly do not know what you are doing" is not.

## Reporting a problem

Email the maintainer at the address in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) if the report is
sensitive, or open an issue if it is not. Reports are read and acted on; you will get an
acknowledgement.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break this standard, and may block
accounts for repeated or severe breaches.

## Attribution

This is a short project-specific standard rather than an adopted template. If you are looking
for a well-tested general code of conduct, the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/)
is the usual choice and this document is compatible with it.
